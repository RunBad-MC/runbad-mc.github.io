🛡️ Claims Guide

Prefer to browse in-game? Run /claimsguide to open this same guide as a menu, or /warp claimstutorial for a walk-through area built to show it off in person.

How to Claim

Create Your First Claim

  • Right-click with a Golden Shovel in opposite corners of your area.
  • Claims automatically extend from bedrock to sky limit.
  • A Gold Block/Glowstone corner marker will appear when successful!

Expand Your Claim

  • Right-click an existing claim corner with a Golden Shovel, then right-click the new location.
  • NOTE: You need enough claim blocks in your balance!

Trust Friends

  • /trust <player> — full build rights
  • /containertrust <player> — Chests/Barrels/Furnaces etc. only
  • /accesstrust <player> — Doors/Buttons only

Basic Claim Commands

  • /abandonclaim — deletes the claim you're standing in
  • /claimslist — shows all your claims and remaining blocks
  • /trustlist — shows who has access to your claim
  • /buyclaimblocks — trade money for extra claim blocks. /warp nook also sells claim block vouchers at a reduced price
  • Claim blocks are also obtainable for free via vouchers — a chance drop from voting or from opening the Sprout Crate

Check Claim Info

  • Right-click anywhere inside a claim with a Stick to see the owner, and claim size.
  • Glowstone and Gold Blocks show the boundary visualization.

Claim Types

Here's what each type looks like from above.

Player Claim

A regular claim owned by a player.

  • Glowstone = corner
  • Gold Block = edge
  • Glass = protected (inside)
  • Grass = unclaimed (outside)

A subdivision was nearby too, so both marker types show up in these shots — see below for what the Subdivision markers mean.

Subdivision

A smaller area inside a larger claim.

  • Iron Block = corner
  • White Wool = edge
  • Glass = protected (inside)
  • Grass = unclaimed (outside)

Admin Claim

Server-owned protected land.

  • Glowstone = corner
  • Pumpkin = edge
  • Glass = protected (inside)
  • Grass = unclaimed (outside)

Locking Individual Blocks & Entities (Bolt)

GriefPrevention claims already protect everything inside them — but only from players you haven't trusted. Bolt solves a different problem: keeping specific chests, doors, and other blocks private even from people you've /trusted on a shared claim. It also works outside of claims entirely, protecting your stuff on unclaimed land too.

Locking & Unlocking

  • /lock then punch the block — lock the block you're looking at (Chests, Barrels, Doors, and more)
  • /unlock then punch the block — remove the lock

Managing Access

  • /edit add <player> then punch the block — give a player access to a locked block
  • /edit remove <player> then punch the block — remove a player's access
  • /bolt transfer <player> then punch the block — hand off ownership of a locked block entirely

Locking is its own trust system, separate from claim trust. Trusting someone on your claim (/trust, /containertrust, /accesstrust) doesn't automatically give them access to something you've /lock'd — manage that separately with /edit.

Blocks Aren't Auto-Protected

Blocks stay unlocked until you /lock them yourself. This covers things like:

  • Storage: Chests, Trapped Chests, Copper Chests, Barrels, Shulker Boxes, Hoppers, Droppers, Dispensers
  • Cooking & Crafting: Furnaces, Blast Furnaces, Smokers, Brewing Stands, Crafters, Cauldrons, Anvils
  • Other: Composters, Beacons, Jukeboxes, Note Blocks, Lecterns, Chiseled Bookshelves, Decorated Pots, Wooden Shelves, Banners, Signs, Doors, Trapdoors, Fence Gates

Exception: Ender Chests are public by default — no lock needed. (Each player's Ender Chest storage is already personal, so this doesn't affect your items.)

Entities Are Automatically Protected

These lock themselves as private the moment you place them — no /lock required:

  • Armor Stands, Item Frames, Glow Item Frames, Paintings, Leash Knots
  • Chest Boats (all wood types) and Bamboo Chest Rafts
  • Chest Minecarts, Furnace Minecarts, Hopper Minecarts
