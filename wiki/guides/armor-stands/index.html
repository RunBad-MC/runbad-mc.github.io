🧍 Armor Stands Guide

Need the perk? The Pose Wand is a one-time perk from the Store. You keep it forever.

Before You Start

You need 2 things:

  • The Pose Wand perk.
  • An armor stand you placed. Stands lock themselves to you when you place them. Only you can pose them (and friends you add, see below).

No armor stand yet? Craft one: 6 sticks and 1 smooth stone slab.

Get the Pose Wand

  1. Type /stands and click Get the Pose Wand. Or type /eas in chat.
  2. You get the Pose Wand (a stick with a little armor stand on top).

Lost it? Type /eas again.

Pose an Arm, Leg, Head or Body

  1. Hold the Pose Wand.
  2. Look at an arm. Grey sticks show up on the stand, and the part you look at gets its name, like Right arm.

  1. Right-click it. Coloured rings show up.
  2. Look at a ring. Right-click and move your mouse. The arm turns.
  3. Right-click again to keep it. Left-click to cancel.

Tip: Left-click goes one step back. Keep left-clicking until nothing is picked.

Move or Turn the Whole Stand

  1. Look at the middle of the stand and right-click. Lines and rings show up around it.
  2. Look at the middle again (it says Move). Right-click and walk. The stand comes with you.
  3. Right-click to place it. Left-click to cancel.

A stand can't go into someone else's claim. It stops at the edge.

The Stand Menu

Pick the whole stand (right-click its middle), then press F.

  • Top left: how to pose (hover it for the steps).
  • Little stands: switches. Green = on, grey = off.
    • Arms
    • Base plate
    • Small stand
  • Outlines: put items on the stand. Click an outline with an item: helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots, sword (right hand), shield (left hand). Click it again to take the item back.

Copy a Pose

  1. Pick a stand you like (right-click its middle).
  2. Type /eas copy.
  3. Pick another of your stands.
  4. Type /eas paste.

Only the pose and the switches are copied. Items are not copied.

Who Can Pose a Stand?

  • You, if you placed it.
  • Friends you add. Type /bolt edit add <name>, then click your stand. Take them off again with /bolt edit remove <name>. They need the perk too.
  • Old stands without a lock: only inside a claim where you can build.
  • Nobody can pose stands at spawn or in other protected places.

Help!

"I can't pick my stand."

  • Is it yours? Look at it and type /bolt info, then click it.
  • Get closer. The wand reaches 10 blocks.
  • Already picked something? Left-click until nothing is picked.

"It says the stand is locked." It belongs to someone else. Ask them to add you: /bolt edit add <your name>.

"It says this stand has no lock." It's an old stand. Lock it first: type /lock, then click it. You can only lock stands where you're allowed to build.

"My stand won't move there." That spot is someone else's claim, or a protected place.

"I lost the Pose Wand." Type /eas.

Locks are explained in the Locks Guide. Claims are in the Claims Guide.
