🛒 Shops Guide

Want to see one first? Run /warp shoptutorial in-game.

Before You Start

You need 3 things:

  • A claim. Your shop must be inside your own claim, or a friend's claim if they /trust you. See the Claims Guide.
  • A barrel. Only barrels can be shops. Chests don't work.
  • The items you want to sell.

Make a Shop

  1. Put down a barrel. Keep the block in front of it empty. The shop sign goes there.
  2. Hold the item you want to sell. How many you hold is how many people get each time they buy. Hold 1 diamond, and people buy 1 diamond. Hold 64 dirt, and people buy 64 dirt.
  3. Sneak (hold Shift) and left-click the barrel.
  4. Chat asks for a price. Type only a number, like 10, and press Enter.

  1. A sign appears on the barrel. Your shop is made! There is no "shop made" message. The sign is how you know.

Fill Your Shop

  1. Right-click the barrel to open it.
  2. Put your items inside.
  3. The sign now says Selling and a number. The number is how many times people can buy, not how many items.

Buy From a Shop

  • Left-click the sign to buy once. It buys right away. There is no "are you sure?".
  • Want more? Sneak + left-click the sign. Then type how many, or all.

Tip: Don't punch your own shop sign. You buy from yourself and your chat fills up.

Change Your Shop

Right-click your shop's sign. A menu opens in chat. Press T to open chat, then click a button:

  • Price [Change]: type the new price.
  • Items per trade [Change]: how many items people get each time they buy. Set the price again after this. It doesn't change by itself!
  • [Switch to buying] or [Switch to selling]: change what your shop does.
  • [Remove Shop]: click it, then click it again within 5 seconds.

Commands work too, while you look at your shop:

  • /qs price 5 sets the price to 5
  • /qs size 16 sells 16 items each time
  • /qs remove removes the shop. Your items stay in the barrel.

Make a Buying Shop

A buying shop pays people for their items.

  1. Make a shop like normal.
  2. Right-click the sign and click [Switch to buying]. The sign now says Buying.
  3. Keep money in your balance. That's what pays people.
  4. Keep empty space in the barrel.

People left-click the sign to sell you their items.

Make a Warp for Your Shop

A player warp lets anyone teleport to your shop. It costs Ⓑ25,000. Bean rank can have 1 warp, and higher ranks can have more (see Ranks).

  1. Stand in front of your shop, facing it. People will arrive right here, looking the same way.
  2. Type /pwarp create MyShop (no spaces in the name).
  3. Click a green tick to pay.

  1. Click HERE in chat to open your warp's settings.
  2. Click the circle ("Change Type of Warp"), then click the shop stall ("Shop"). Now your warp is in the Shops list!

  1. Want more? The card ("Change Description") sets a description. The item frame sets the icon (type hand to use the item you're holding).

You can't make a warp in spawn, the tutorial world, or the resource world.

Find Shops

  • Type /pwarp and click the shop stall (Shops). Click a shop to go there.
  • Know the name? Type /pwarp ShopName.
  • /shop is the server shop at spawn, not player shops.

Help! Something's Wrong

  • Nothing happens when I click the barrel. Are you sneaking? Is it a barrel, not a chest? Are you inside your own claim? Outside a claim, nothing happens and there is no message.
  • "You need [/trust] in this claim to make a shop here." It's your friend's claim. They need to /trust you. /containertrust is not enough.
  • "No room for the sign." Clear the block in front of the barrel.
  • "Numbers only, like 10." Type just the number: 10, not 10 coins.
  • "Only the owner can open this shop." That's normal. Only the owner can open a shop barrel.
  • My warp isn't in the Shops list. Type /pwarp manage MyShop, click the circle, and pick the shop stall.
  • How do I delete my warp? Type /pwarp manage MyShop, click the red no-entry sign (Remove Warp), and confirm. You don't get money back.
  • How do I remove my shop? Look at it and type /qs remove. Your items stay in the barrel.
