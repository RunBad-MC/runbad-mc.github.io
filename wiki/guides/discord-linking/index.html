🔗 Linking Your Discord

Hang out with other Beans outside the server too! Linking takes less than a minute:

  1. Run /discord link in-game. You'll get a 4-digit code.
  2. DM the code to @BeanLink on Discord.
  3. That's it — you're linked!

What You Unlock

  • Ability to send links/media in Discord
  • Access to 💬┇ingame-chat and 🔢┇counting
  • Vote reminders in 📈┇vote-leaderboard
  • Access to voice channels
  • Your in-game rank syncs to Discord automatically!
