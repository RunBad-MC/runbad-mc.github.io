🏳️‍🌈 Elytra Skins

Put a pride flag on your own elytra. It stays the same elytra: same enchants, same wear, same name, and you can still repair it.

How to get a skin

  1. Get a Blank Elytra Skin from the Cosmetics crate at /warp crates.
  2. Put the blank and the flag's dyes in any crafting table, in any order. That makes the flag's skin voucher.
  3. Right-click the voucher twice to unlock the skin for good. Or keep the voucher to trade or sell it, for example on /ah.

The Runbad skin also comes from the vote crate, as a voucher.

Forgot a recipe? Type /skins in-game and click a flag. It shows the recipe in a crafting grid.

All recipes

Every recipe is one Blank Elytra Skin plus these items.

Rainbow

Red, orange, yellow, green, blue and purple dye.

Transgender

2 light blue dye, 2 pink dye and 1 white dye.

Runbad

Cocoa beans, blue dye, magenta dye and white dye.

2 orange dye, white dye, pink dye and magenta dye.

Gay

Cyan, lime, white, light blue and blue dye.

Bisexual

2 magenta dye, 1 purple dye and 2 blue dye.

Pansexual

Pink, yellow and light blue dye.

Nonbinary

Yellow, white, purple and black dye.

Genderfluid

Pink, white, purple, black and blue dye.

Agender

Black, light gray, white and lime dye.

Asexual

Black, light gray, white and purple dye.

Aromantic

Green, lime, white, light gray and black dye.

Biromantic

Magenta, purple, blue and white dye. Its two hearts stand upright while you glide.

How to use a skin

  1. Hold or wear your elytra.
  2. Type /skins.
  3. Click a flag. No skin takes it off again.

The flag stays on the elytra when you trade, sell or store it.

Have an old pride elytra?

Hold it and type /skins. You can swap it for a normal elytra with that flag. You keep its wear, its enchantments and its name, and the skin is yours for good.

Questions

  • I got a voucher for a skin I already have. Redeeming it just tells you that you have the skin. You keep the voucher, so you can trade or sell it.
  • I can't open a crafting table with a voucher in my hand. It opens normally. To redeem while looking at a crafting table, chest or door, sneak and right-click.
  • I use JEI or REI and can't find the recipes. Those mods don't know server recipes. Use /skins in-game or this page. The normal recipe book has them.
  • Can I use a skin on more than one elytra? Yes. Once a skin is yours, you can put it on any of your elytras.
