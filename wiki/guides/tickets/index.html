🎫 Tickets Guide

A ticket is a private chat with staff. Only you and staff can see it. Tickets ping all staff, so use one when it really needs staff:

  • Player Report: someone is breaking the rules, or is starting arguments or trying to get a rise out of people.
  • Bug: something is broken, or something exploded and you're scared.
  • Help: you're stuck and need staff to fix something.
  • Redeem: you have a voucher that needs staff to finish it, like a custom elytra skin or a custom join/leave message.
  • Other: anything else that needs staff.

For less important things, ask in #quick-support on our Discord.

Not for: partnering, hiring, or offering us a server host. Tickets like that, or anything trying to get around this rule, get a ban and no reply.

Open a Ticket

  1. Type /ticket (or open /menu and click Help Tickets).
  2. Pick what it's about.
  3. Say clearly why you're making the ticket, then press Enter.

Staff get a ping, in game and on Discord.

Reporting a Player

  • Include evidence: screenshots, clips, chat logs. We can't act on "someone told me..." or one witness. We need something concrete.
  • Please don't call people out or argue in public chat. Make a ticket and we'll handle it. ❤️

Reporting a Bug

  • Tell us what happened, what you expected to happen, and anything that helps us make it happen again.
  • Bug reports make the Pantry better. Sometimes there's a thank-you for it!

Appeals

  • Appeal a punishment in a ticket, never in public chat. Tickets are the safe, private place, and we review things fairly.
  • Banned and can't join? Make a ticket on our Discord.

Talk to Staff

Right after you open a ticket, everything you type goes to your ticket, not to public chat.

  • Type done to go back to normal chat.
  • /ticket chat takes you back in.
  • /ticket reply <message> sends one line without switching.

Staff replies show up in your chat. Missed one while offline? You'll be told when you log in.

See or Close Your Tickets

  • /ticket shows your open tickets.
  • /ticket close closes yours when you're done.

You can have 2 open at once.

Emergency?

On our Discord you may ping all staff with the staff role.
