🔒 Locks Guide

Claims or Locks?

  • A claim keeps out everyone you didn't /trust. See the Claims Guide.
  • A lock keeps one thing private, even from people you trusted. It also works outside claims.

You don't need a lock inside your claim. Use one when you share a claim and want to keep one chest just for you.

What Locks Itself

These lock to you the moment you place them:

  • Armor stands, item frames, glow item frames, paintings, leads on fences
  • Chest boats, chest rafts, furnace minecarts

Everything else (chests, barrels, doors, furnaces, shulker boxes...) stays unlocked until you lock it.

Lock Something

  1. Type /lock.
  2. Click the thing.

You can only lock things in places where you can build: the wild, your own claim, or a claim you're trusted in.

Unlock It

  1. Type /unlock.
  2. Click it.

Let a Friend Use It

  1. Type /bolt edit add <name>.
  2. Click the thing.

Take them off again: /bolt edit remove <name>, then click it.

Who Owns This?

  1. Type /bolt info.
  2. Click it. Chat shows the owner and who else is allowed.

Shared Locks

Add a word after /lock to let other people in a little:

  • /lock display: everyone can look inside, only you can take.
  • /lock deposit: everyone can put things in, only you can take out.
  • /lock withdrawal: everyone can take things out.
  • /lock public: everyone can use it, but only you can break it or unlock it.

Give It Away

  1. Type /bolt transfer <name>, then click it.
  2. They type /bolt requests accept <your name>.

Help!

"It says: This Chest is locked." It belongs to someone else. Ask them to add you: /bolt edit add <your name>.

"You can't lock this here." It's in someone else's claim or a protected place.

"How do I lock a shulker box?" Place it, type /lock, click it. Picked it up again? Lock it again after you place it.
