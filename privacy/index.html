Privacy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Who is responsible

Alexah Mae Woods owns and runs Runbad and is responsible for the data described here. Contact: support@runbad.net. Runbad is not run by Mojang or Microsoft.

What we keep

  • Game account: your Minecraft username and UUID, your rank and permissions.
  • Connection data: your IP address, used for security, enforcing bans and spotting ban evasion.
  • Play data: playtime and sessions, and the approximate country your connection comes from (not your exact location).
  • Moderation logs: block and container changes and commands, so staff can fix griefing. Block logs are deleted after 180 days.
  • Discord: if you link your account, your Discord ID and name.
  • Votes and polls: your username with the votes and answers you give.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Tebex, which keeps your payment and purchase history under its own privacy policy. We receive your username and what you bought, so we can give you the item. We never see your card details.

This website

The website has no accounts and does not run its own advertising or tracking scripts. It is served through GitHub Pages and Cloudflare, which can process ordinary technical request data such as your IP address.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising.

Your choices

Email support@runbad.net to ask what we hold about you or to ask us to delete it. We will remove what we reasonably can. We may keep records we need for safety or to enforce a ban.

Age

Runbad is for players aged 13 and over. If we learn that someone under 13 is playing, we remove their data.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
