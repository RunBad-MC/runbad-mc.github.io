Rules

Updated July 2026

These rules apply in-game and in Discord.

Most issues are solved by: be kind, play fair, respect builds, keep chat cozy.


🤝 Be Kind

Treat everyone kindly.

  • - No hate speech, slurs, harassment, dogpiling, or targeted insults.
  • - Respect names/pronouns/identities and personal boundaries.
  • - Light swearing is fine — just not at someone.
  • - Issues? Open a ticket on Discord and let staff handle it.

Examples (not exhaustive):

  • - ✅ "lol that was messed up" / ❌ "you're stupid"
  • - ✅ "please don't @ me for this" / ❌ repeatedly pushing someone's boundary

⚔️ Keep It Fair

Everyone gets the same beans; play fair.

  • - No hacks, x-ray, macros, autoclickers, or noclip.
  • - No freecam, at any time. This includes camera mods bundled in modpacks.
  • - Nothing may progress while you're not physically there — no auto-builders or auto-placement mods.
  • - AFK-holding a key (e.g. fishing) is fine, but come back within 30 minutes or it counts as unattended farming.
  • - No abusing bugs/glitches/dupes/exploits — please report them via ticket instead.
  • - Don't lag the Pantry: avoid massive redstone/mob farms.

Notes:

  • - HUDs, Minimaps, REI/JEI are OK.
  • - Building on the roof of the Nether is completely fine.
  • - If you're unsure whether a mod/client feature is allowed, ask staff first.
  • - "I found a dupe" is not a flex — it's a ticket.

💬 Chat Rules

Keep chat PG-13 and friendly.

  • - No spam, floods, or excessive caps.
  • - No advertising other servers, your plugin/mod/paid service, or "sponsoring the server." This includes DMs, and Shared Worlds like Essential Mod.
  • - No begging for handouts. Asking for help is fine. Badgering and harassing others to give you free stuff, is not.
  • - Global/in-game chat stays light (no heavy real-life topics or controversial debates).
  • - Use #off-topic for memes + tangents, and #serious-beans-18 (opt-in) for heavier adult conversation within the rules.
  • - You can meme. Just don't be mean.

🏡 Respect Builds & Space

No griefing, stealing, scamming, trapping, or TP-kills.

  • - Build at least 200 blocks from other players.
  • - Respect claims and other people's builds.
  • - Ask permission before building near someone's area.
  • - If you can see it (visually or on minimap), you're too close.
  • - Don't spam /tpa — if asked to stop, stop.
  • - 1x1 towers count as griefing unless built in a Resource world.
  • - Unclaimed doesn't mean free-for-all. Don't steal, destroy, or modify something that isn't yours. If unsure, ask.
  • - Claim your builds. Unclaimed builds won't be rolled back or repaired by staff if griefed or stolen from — the griefer still breaks the rules and can still be punished, but the damage stays. Claims exist to protect you; use them.

Notes:

  • - "But it wasn't claimed" doesn't automatically make it okay if it's clearly someone's work.
  • - When in doubt: ask, or pick a different spot.

🧾 Reporting, Staff & Enforcement

  • - Emergency? Ping us with @Staff.
  • - If something's wrong: report it in #create-a-ticket (screenshots/logs help a lot).
  • - Don't mini-mod. Friendly reminders are okay; otherwise let staff handle it.
  • - No witch hunts. Don't publicly accuse or call out players for breaking rules — report it privately and let staff look into it.
  • - Staff may make judgment calls for edge cases to keep things consistent.
  • - If you disagree with a decision, open a ticket — please don't argue it out in chat.

How actions usually work:

  • - Most things: reminder → warning → escalation if it continues.
  • - Serious stuff (e.g., intentional cheating or griefing): may escalate quickly, including no-warning action when appropriate.

By playing on the Runbad Minecraft server or using our Discord, you agree to follow Mojang/Microsoft's Terms of Use, the Minecraft EULA, and Discord's Terms of Service & Community Guidelines wherever they apply. Our house rules sit on top of those.
