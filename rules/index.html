Rules

Updated November 8, 2025

TL;DR

  1. Be Kind. No harassment, hate, bigotry, or creepy behavior — ever.
  2. Keep it PG‑13. Don’t be gross; keep NSFW out of public spaces.
  3. No cheating or dupes. Client mods are fine if they’re performance/QoL only. Anything that automates gameplay or reveals hidden info = nope.
  4. Don’t grief or steal. Respect people’s stuff — ask before you “borrow,” unclaimed builds are NOT fair game.
  5. Ask permission before building near someone else’s area! If you can see it visually or via minimap, you’re too close. 150-200 blocks minimum
  6. Keep lag in check. Be thoughtful with farms/redstone. Staff may pause/adjust things that go wonky.
  7. Resource World resets monthly. Don’t build anything you love there. Use
    /rtp to hop around.
  8. PvP is consent‑only unless you’ve opted‑in. No trap TPs or combat logging. Use /pvp to toggle.
  9. Public space, mixed ages. Treat in-game chat and Discord like a shared public space with both kids and adults. Keep things broadly PG-13.
  10. Fair trades only. No scamming, IRL money deals, or auction shenanigans.
  11. Listen to staff. If we ask you to change something, please do. Staff have final say on all matters.
  12. Report issues with /reportplayer or on Discord.

1. Respect & Safety

• Zero tolerance for harassment, hate speech, slurs, threats, doxxing, stalking, or discriminatory behavior.

• Keep public spaces (in‑game chat, signs, books, Discord public channels) PG‑13.

• Runbad is a mixed-age community (teens and adults). Public spaces should feel safe and welcoming for younger players and marginalized folks.

• Don’t impersonate other players or staff.

• Consent applies to everything social — if someone asks you to stop, stop.

2. Chat & Communications

• No spam, ASCII walls, or mic-spam in Discord voice channels.

• Global chat (in-game and in #general) should stay light and broadly PG-13. Remember: this is a children’s block game with kids and adults sharing the same space.

• Avoid heavy real-world topics and hot-button debates in global chat (politics, religion, graphic news, real-world tragedy, etc.). Most people are here to escape that stuff — use local chat, /msg, or Discord DMs instead.

• Oversharing / venting about serious topics (health, trauma, finances, etc.) should not happen in global chat. If you opt into the #serious-beans-18 channel on Discord, you may discuss heavier topics there, but you must still be respectful and follow Discord’s Community Guidelines.

• If staff ask you to move or drop a topic, please do so. Debating the rule in public chat will be treated as a separate chat issue.

• DO NOT advertise or otherwise promote other Minecraft servers, or Discord communities.

• Nicknames: fun formatting is okay, but keep it legible and non-misleading (no staff tags, no copycat names).

3. Cheating, Clients & Mods

Disallowed:

  • • X-ray
  • • Autoclickers of any speed are banned
  • • Any program or button that produces a CPS (click per second) of above 20 is also banned
  • • Buttons or programs that allow you to produce multiple click outputs from one click or interaction
  • • Fly or hover
  • • NoFall
  • • Container Finder
  • • Reach
  • • Aim, forcefield, or killaura
  • • Anything that gives you an unfair advantage over other players
  • If you’re unsure: ask first.

4. Claims, Grief & Pranks

  • • Claim before you build. Unclaimed DOES NOT free to grief.
  • Ask permission before building near someone else’s area! If you can see it visually or via minimap, you’re too close. 150-200 blocks minimum
  • • Don’t alter or loot builds without permission, even if they look abandoned.
  • • Pranks are okay if harmless, reversible, and cleaned up within a reasonable time. No lava, TNT, or mob bombs.
  • • If something goes missing, ping staff and we’ll check logs.

    • Visit /warp claimstutorial if you’re new to GriefPrevention!

5. Economy, Shops & Trades

  • • No scamming, chargebacks, or IRL money deals.
  • • Be clear with prices, quantities, and enchant levels.
  • • Auction/Shop rules: no bait‑and‑switch, no junk‑filling, no “fee traps.”
  • Example: selling fake items (lored fake enchants, dirt renamed to "Diamond", etc.) without disclosing that the item is fake.

    • Staff may reverse trades or refund Ⓑ (Bean Bucks) for clear scams.

6. Farms, Redstone & Performance

Do:

  • • Add off switches and overflow protection.
  • • Use water streams instead of hopper carpets where possible.
  • • Space villagers/mobs across chunks; consolidate storage.
  • • Keep pillager, piglin, or raid contraptions from snowballing.

Don't:

  • • Run intentionally laggy clocks or chunk‑eating machines.
  • • Use TNT dupers, bedrock breaking, or illicit portal tricks.
  • • Create hostile mob swarms that pin MSPT to the floor.

If something starts cooking the TPS, staff may pause, trim, relocate, or remove the offending bits and message you with next steps.

7. Worlds & Land Use

  • • Overworld/Nether/End: general survival etiquette applies. Protect public travel lines and community builds.
  • • Resource World (resets monthly): mine, chop, and dig to your heart’s content — but don’t build anything you want to keep. Use /rtp to explore.
  • • Withers & other boss fights: take them into the resource world, please.

8. PvP & Traps

  • • Consent‑only PvP outside of designated/opt‑in areas. Use /pvp to toggle if you want to participate.
  • • No teleport traps, nether portal traps, or forced suffocation mechanics.
  • • No combat logging (quitting to avoid death) once a fight is clearly underway.

9. AFK, Alts & Accounts

  • • AFK is fine; AFK farming that plays the game for you is not.
  • • One alternate (alt) account per person is allowed on the server for purposes such as farm loading.
  • YOU are responsible for anything done on accounts you control.

Alt account prohibitions

  • • Using an alt account to evade punishments.
  • • Having more than one alt account online at one time per person.
  • • Using an alt and a VPN to double vote.
  • • Using or encouraging the use of an alt generator.
  • • Using Stolen, bought or another player’s account (against EULA)

10. Staff Direction & Disputes

  • • If staff asks you to adjust something (a farm, a chat behavior, a build location), please do so. We’ll explain the why.

  • Disagree? That’s okay — open a polite thread and we’ll review.

  • Do not harass staff or drag fights across channels.

11. Enforcement Ladder (typical)

  • • We use judgment first. Most first‑time, low‑impact issues → quick verbal nudge (unlogged) if it solves the problem.
  • • If behavior repeats or the impact is bigger: Warning → Short mute/timeout → Temp ban → Perm ban.
  • • Immediate action for clear line‑crossers: hate/bigotry, doxxing, major grief/theft, obvious cheats, evasion.
  • • Discord: open a ticket or DM a staff member if it’s sensitive (screenshots help).
  • • Appeals: be honest, own mistakes, and propose a path forward. We like second chances.

12. Reporting & Appeals

  • In‑game: /reportplayer with a short description. Staff can see even if not online at the time.

13. Change Log & Rule Updates

  • • Runbad is currently alpha. Stuff will break, shift, or get rebalanced — we’ll try to announce major changes.
  • • This Rules page always reflects the current policy; significant updates will be summarized in #announcements on Discord.

By playing on the Runbad Minecraft server or using our Discord, you agree to follow Mojang/Microsoft’s Terms of Use, the Minecraft EULA, and Discord’s Terms of Service & Community Guidelines wherever they apply. Our house rules sit on top of those.
