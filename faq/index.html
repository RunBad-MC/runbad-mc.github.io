FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Runbad a PvP server?

No. PvP is optional and off by default. This is a PvE-focused survival server.

How many players are usually online?

Usually 2–10. Runbad is intentionally small and quiet — not a busy hub.

Do I need to claim my builds?

Yes. Griefing and stealing are against the rules either way — but only claimed builds (Golden Shovel/GriefPrevention) get restored by staff if something happens to them.

How long has Runbad been running?

Since October 2025.

Is there a whitelist?

No whitelist currently — anyone can join and play.

How do I link my Discord?

Use /discord link in-game to connect your account.

Is Runbad LGBTQ+ friendly?

Yes. Runbad is a safe space for LGBTQ+ players.

Is there an age requirement?

Yes. Runbad is a 13+ server.

What's at spawn?

Leaderboards, community shared storage, anvils, and an enchanting table — all ready to use right away.
